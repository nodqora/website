One edition exists. This is the published line the second one will be drawn on.

Today Nodqora has one edition: Community, Apache-2.0. Enterprise is not built and is not for sale. There is no proprietary distribution, no licence key, and no trial to arrange. Nothing on this page can be bought.

It is published anyway, because a line between editions is worth more as a commitment made before there is anything to sell than as one announced alongside a price. Everything below is decided; most of it is not built, on either side.

Community is the public repository, Apache-2.0. Enterprise will be a separate proprietary distribution; the public repository never contains proprietary files.

No capability shipped in Community ever moves to Enterprise. Every Community row below is a commitment, not a placement. Capabilities may move the other way — Enterprise to Community — and the revisit triggers behind this page operate only in that direction. The Community edition stays Apache-2.0 and self-hostable, which closes the manoeuvre where a vendor never demotes a feature but withdraws the free way to run it.

How the line is drawn

Placements are made by two rules applied in order, and by nothing else.

  • The split is buyer-based. What one engineer or team needs to see the truth of their system is Community. What an organisation needs to run it across many teams or over time is Enterprise.
  • The query is free; the standing query is paid. Any question a user asks of the graph in front of them is observation, and observation is Community. The same question evaluated on a schedule, watched for change and reported when it changes, is continuous monitoring, and that is Enterprise.

Anything not listed is Community by default, except four categories that are Enterprise by standing rule: state flowing outward to source systems; approval and request workflow; continuous monitoring and alerting; and rollups aggregating more than one Nodqora install.

How Enterprise will be sold

The terms below are decided and published in advance of having anything to charge for, so that a Community user can tell today what a paid edition would and would not cost them. Nothing here is chargeable yet.

A flat annual subscription per installation. The unit is a deployment of Nodqora, and nothing Nodqora observes is ever counted. There is exactly one countable object and it is the thing you deploy, not the estate you own.

Replicas of one deploymentOne installation. Scale out freely
Non-production copies — sandbox, upgrade rehearsal, config testingFree and unlimited
A DR standbyFree while passive; an installation once it serves users
Environments inside one installUnlimited and free, on both editions
Discovered nodes, edges, plugins, connected systemsUnlimited and free, on both editions
Users who sign inUnlimited and free, on both editions

You would receive one key per installation, carrying a name you choose — eu-prod, acme-gov-enclave. Nothing fingerprints your hardware or your cluster. The key verifies offline and never contacts us; an air-gapped install stays air-gapped.

If a subscription lapses, nothing stops working. The install boots, the canvas renders, health refreshes, the journal keeps writing, and every view scope already configured stays enforced. What freezes is the control path — new roles, scopes, monitoring rules, policies and retention changes — after a thirty-day grace period past expiry. The key is a renewal prompt, not a lock, and it is sold as one.

The trial

Thirty days, full-featured, arranged through us — once there is a build to trial. Extended by reissuing a key if a security review or procurement runs long.

It will not be self-serve, for a structural reason worth stating plainly: Enterprise is a private artifact, and possession of that artifact is what gates capability. A self-serve download would be a decision to publish the proprietary build to anyone who fills in a form.

The price

There is no price, because there is nothing to sell. Nor will a list price be published the day there is: not until there is enough selling behind it for a published number to be something other than a guess — and a published guess is an anchor we would own permanently.

The commitment is to publish a list price once five Enterprise deals have closed, or twelve months of active selling have passed, whichever comes first. The unit and the trial terms are published above, so you can tell what you would be charged for without a call.

There is no free Enterprise tier, at any threshold. Community is the answer for a small organisation, and it is a complete one.

Support

Only the Community channel exists today — public issues, described at the bottom of this section. The Enterprise terms are stated first because they are the ones a buyer would need to evaluate, not because they are on offer.

Support will be bundled into the Enterprise subscription. There is no separate support product, no tier ladder and no add-on. It runs with an active term — trials included — and ends at expiry.

What Enterprise would get

One business day to first response, Monday to Friday, UTC+4.

That is the whole commitment. There is no restoration or resolution commitment, no severity ladder and nothing 24x7 — because there is one maintainer, and a commitment that cannot be kept on a bad week is not worth publishing. The timezone is stated rather than hidden behind "business hours" so you can convert it before you buy: from US Pacific, expect an answer overnight.

Two things bound what an outage costs you. Nodqora observes; it does not serve traffic — nothing in your estate stops when Nodqora stops. And a lapsed key does not cause one.

Diagnosis is included even where the fix is yours. A plugin showing blind is usually RBAC, a network policy or a credential — and finding out which is work we do before we can tell you, so we commit to doing it. What we do not do is reach into your cluster and change it.

Licence, key reissue and renewal questions are answered for anyone, at any time, in or out of term. A frozen control path you want unfrozen is not a support ticket.

What Community gets — the channel that exists today

Public issues on the repository. No response commitment, and no private channel. Every question and every answer is in public, where the next person finds it — which is the only shape one maintainer can serve, and the only one where an answer is worth more than once.

Nothing is promised about how fast a Community issue is answered, and that is deliberate rather than evasive: in practice every one gets read, and promising that would turn a habit into a term.

A paid support agreement against Community is possible and is not a tier. It has no published price and no threshold. If you want assurance and no Enterprise capability, ask.
